Quick questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes — everything saves automatically on your device. Close the page and come back; your points, routes and logs will be waiting. The footer shows exactly where your progress is stored.

Can I change my habits mid-month?

Absolutely. Edit your flight plan anytime, then tap “Apply plan to all un-logged days” to roll the changes forward. Days you've already flown keep their results.

Do I have to fly the days in order?

Not at all. Log any day, any time, in any order that suits your life. Routes open in network order (quiet frontiers first), no matter which day you fly.

What if I want to start over?

Use “Reset day to plan” for a single day, or “Reset the whole season” in the footer for a clean slate. No guilt, no penalty.
